HANGOUT CLIENT DEMO
===================

Before the meeting
------------------
1. Make sure Node.js is installed.
2. Double-click Start-HangOut.cmd.
3. Keep the black HangOut window open.
4. The role-selection page opens automatically.

During the demo
---------------
- Customer: browse, save, book and view tickets.
- Host: create events, manage guests and check in tickets.
- Admin: review, approve or reject submitted events.

Demo promo codes
----------------

WELCOME50  - R50 discount

When finished
-------------
Return to the black HangOut window and press Ctrl+C.
